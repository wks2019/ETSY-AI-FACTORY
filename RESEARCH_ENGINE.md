# PURPOSE

The Research Engine identifies the highest-value digital products to create.

Its objective is to maximize:

• Revenue
• Conversion
• Profit
• Evergreen sales
• Bundle opportunities
• Customer satisfaction

Research always comes before design.

Never create products without validating demand.

# CORE PRINCIPLE

The factory does not build products.

The factory solves customer problems.

Every product must answer a real customer need.

If no problem exists

Do not build the product.

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

Research is complete only when Claude can answer:

Who is the customer?

What problem are we solving?

Why will they buy this?

Why is ours better?

The Research Engine determines the future of the ETSY-AI-FACTORY.

Always understand the customer, validate demand, analyze competitors, identify opportunities, and document findings before production begins.
